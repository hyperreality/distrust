Method
------
For each domain, https://<domain>/ is tried, then https://www.<domain>/.
The first one that verifies with this host's normal CA store (Debian) is then
fetched again with curl trusting only the 30 allowed roots (no CA path).
A site whose chain does not reach an allowed root fails. Timeouts were retried.
"Anchor" is the first allowed root OpenSSL reaches in the chain the server sends;
browsers also stop at the first trusted certificate, so a site that serves a
newer root cross-signed by an older one counts under the newer root.

UNREACHABLE means neither name answered HTTPS with the normal CA store (API,
CDN and tracking domains without a website, or sites blocking scripted
clients). These say nothing about the policy and are left out of the totals.
